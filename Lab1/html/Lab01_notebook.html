Interpretation

TODO three to six sentences in your own words. Why must c0 and eta0 be computed from eps0 and mu0 rather than typed in as numbers, and why is == the wrong way to compare a computed field value against a formula.

1. Since c0 and eta0 are constants, it is important to have as much precision as possible, especially when many significant digits are needed. Using a number instead of a function will introduce rounding errors that can significantly alter results. 2. The comparison operator "==" is not effective for comparing a computed field value against a formula because floating point numbers calculated by the system may not match the formula exactly due to error, and thus using "==" will evaluate the statement to "False."
